THE CONTEXT BEHIND THE NUMBERS

Built around hourly history.

A small forecasting workspace backed by the Phase 1 project.

A forecast starts before the form.

The models use recent pollution, trailing summaries, calendar patterns, and weather. A single set of current readings is not enough on its own: it is combined with the preceding hours to make a one-hour forecast.

01 / THE DATA

Five years in Beijing.

The UCI Beijing PM2.5 dataset contains 43,824 hourly rows from 2010–2014. PM2.5 was measured at the US Embassy; weather comes from Beijing Capital International Airport.

View the original dataset
02 / THE EXPERIMENT

Time stays in order.

Initial training and EDA use 2010–2012. Settings are selected on 2013. The final comparison uses four expanding quarterly backtests in 2014, with the settings held fixed.

03 / YOUR INPUTS

Know your starting point.

In the form, a new hour is added to the selected history. Sample inputs repeat the last available measurements as editable example values. These are historical scenarios, not live Beijing readings.

An uploaded CSV instead predicts from its last observed hour. You can then add another reading using the form.

04 / THE LIMITS

Keep the context with it.

This historical Beijing study does not establish accuracy in another city or today. Weather availability at forecast time is assumed. The outputs are hourly concentrations; no AQI categories or prediction intervals are calculated.

The recent window stays in this browser tab and travels with your forecast request. Temporary upload files are removed after validation; no shared upload session is required.
